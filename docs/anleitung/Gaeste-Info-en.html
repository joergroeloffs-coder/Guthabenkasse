Your Balance Card

How it works – and how you get your remaining balance back
Whatever you don't spend is paid back to you at the end of the evening.

Just bring your card to the till at the bar: the remaining balance will be paid out to you.

Get your card and top up

Pay any amount you like at the till. It is credited to your card and you see your balance straight away. You can top up again at any time.

Pay with your card

Order as usual and hold your card up at the bar. The staff scan the QR code; the screen shows at the top what was charged and at the bottom what is left on the card.

Check your balance

Ask at the bar for your balance at any time. Scanning alone costs nothing; nothing is charged.

Tip – optional

After paying, the staff can deduct a tip from your card if you like (for example 5 % or 10 %, rounded to 0.50 €).

Money back

At the end of the evening hand in your card at the till at the bar. Whatever is still on it is paid out to you.

Keep it safeThe card is like cash: whoever has it can use it. Please do not hand it over, and do not photograph the QR code.
Lost it?Tell us at the till straight away. We block the card; the team will work out with you what can be done about the balance.
Something wrong?Wrong charge? Tell us at once – bookings can be reversed. If the balance is not enough, simply top up.
Please keep the QR code clean and unfolded so that it can be read.
